HOW TO PLAY

AIM — drag on the lane, use ← / → keys, or follow the glowing guide line.
POSITION — A / D keys shift your starting spot on the approach.
POWER — use the slider, scroll wheel, or hold on the lane to charge (it oscillates — release at the right moment).
SPIN — the spin slider adds hook. The ball curves more as it travels down-lane.
THROW — release your drag, press SPACE, or hit the THROW button.
BUMPERS — bouncing bumper gutters are ON by default. Toggle them in Settings → Gameplay.
STRIKES — knock down all ten pins on your first throw and the alley rains confetti. 🎉
COINS — every strike pays 10 ◉ and every spare pays 5 ◉. They keep adding up between games.
UPGRADES — spend 50 ◉ in the shop to roll 2 balls at once, and another 50 for each extra ball after that (up to 6).

Strikes, spares and the 10th-frame bonus are scored by the rules. Everything you customize — ball, pins, costumes, lane, neon signs — is saved automatically.
